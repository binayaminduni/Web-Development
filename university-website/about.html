About Us

Edinburgh National University was founded in 2001. We believe in learning with confidence.

We have over 10,000 students and 500 teachers. Our campus is in Edinburgh, with modern classrooms, library, computer labs and sports grounds.

Our Vision

To be the best university for students who want to learn, grow and build a great future.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education, practical skills and confidence for every student.
